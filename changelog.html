iQWEB Changelog

A versioned record of visible iQWEB improvements, focused on changes that affect report clarity, trust, presentation, and the overall experience of using the product.

Visit Main Page
Release Log

User-facing changes only.

This changelog tracks product changes that matter to agencies, consultants, and evaluators looking at iQWEB as a real workflow tool. Entries are written in terms of report clarity, signal coverage, trust improvements, and practical product quality.

v1.0 · Feb 2026

Initial public release

iQWEB launched as a public website diagnostics platform designed to turn PageSpeed Insights, Core Web Vitals, and related technical signals into structured reports agencies can use when explaining issues to clients.

  • Performance, SEO, accessibility and security diagnostics
  • Client-friendly report explanations instead of raw metrics
  • Structured issue prioritisation for easier client conversations
  • Shareable report format designed for agencies and consultants
v1.1 · Feb 2026

Report clarity improvements

Improved how diagnostic findings are explained so reports are easier for non-technical clients to understand.

  • Clearer issue descriptions in report sections
  • Improved prioritisation of key problems
  • Better explanation of PageSpeed and Core Web Vitals signals
v1.2 · Mar 2026

Dashboard and scan flow refinements

Improved the overall experience of running scans and accessing reports.

  • More reliable scan handling and status feedback
  • Improved messaging around scan availability
  • Cleaner navigation between scan, dashboard and report views
v1.3 · Mar 2026

Pricing and product messaging improvements

Refined how iQWEB pricing and report usage are explained so agencies evaluating the tool can understand value faster.

  • Clearer pricing structure
  • Improved explanation of report usage for agencies
  • Better alignment between product messaging and report output
v1.4 · Mar 2026

White-label reporting for agencies

Introduced white-label report support so agencies can present iQWEB diagnostics directly to clients under their own branding.

  • Agency logo support in report header
  • Custom agency name and footer information
  • Reports can now be presented without visible iQWEB branding
  • Improved report layout to support branded presentation
  • Updated pricing tiers to clearly introduce white-label capability
  • Landing page messaging aligned with agency client-report workflows
v1.5 · Mar 2026

Agency positioning and report workflow updates

Refined product positioning to better reflect how agencies actually use diagnostics when communicating with clients.

  • Updated homepage messaging to emphasise client-ready reports
  • Improved explanation of how diagnostics translate into agency deliverables
  • Better alignment between product messaging and agency workflows
v1.6 · Mar 2026

Platform-aware diagnostics and security clarity improvements

Improved how iQWEB handles platform-managed environments (such as hosted builders and managed infrastructure) to remove misleading security warnings and ensure reports reflect what is actually actionable.

  • Platform-managed security signals are no longer treated as actionable issues
  • Security headers controlled by hosting platforms are clearly labelled as platform-managed
  • Security scoring is normalised to prevent false negatives in hosted environments
  • Security signals are excluded from Top Issues and primary constraint selection when not actionable
  • Signal Evidence now reflects platform-managed context instead of implying missing implementation
  • Key Insights exclude platform-controlled signals to maintain actionable recommendations
  • Overall report consistency improved to reduce contradictory or confusing outputs
v1.7 · Mar 2026

Colour customisation for stronger brand alignment

Added colour customisation support so agencies can better align both on-screen reports and exported PDFs with their own brand presentation.

  • Custom brand colour support added for on-screen report presentation
  • PDF exports now reflect selected branding colours for better consistency
  • Improved visual alignment between agency identity and client-facing reports
  • Branded reports now feel more polished and presentation-ready
v1.8 · Mar 2026

Security & Trust scoring rebalanced for real-world accuracy

Following additional testing and user feedback, the Security & Trust score was found to be overly harsh for otherwise standard, well-functioning websites. This has now been refined to better reflect real-world conditions while still highlighting meaningful improvements.

  • Introduced a score floor for HTTPS-enabled sites to prevent unfairly low scores
  • Rebalanced header weighting to reflect optional hardening rather than critical failure
  • Security is now only treated as a primary constraint when genuinely severe
  • Updated report language to present header gaps as improvements, not risks
  • Improved detection logic for more consistent and accurate reporting
v1.9 · Apr 2026

AI Visibility diagnostics introduced

iQWEB now includes AI visibility diagnostics to help agencies understand how websites are interpreted by modern AI systems and generative search environments.

  • New AI Visibility diagnostic category added to reports
  • Detection of signals that influence how AI systems interpret site structure and content
  • Improved explanation of how technical signals affect AI-driven discovery
  • Reports now reflect the combined impact of SEO, performance, and AI visibility
  • Product messaging updated to position iQWEB as an SEO and AI visibility diagnostics platform
v1.10 · Apr 2026

Baseline comparison for scan history

Introduced baseline support in the dashboard so agencies can compare future scans against a selected reference report for the same website.

  • Scan History now allows selecting a report as the comparison baseline
  • Future scans are evaluated relative to the chosen baseline
  • Clear Baseline option added to reset comparison state
  • Baseline guide added to the dashboard for quick user guidance
  • Improves tracking of performance and diagnostics changes over time
v1.11 · Apr 2026

Report comparison and PDF output improvements

Improved how iQWEB reports present historical comparisons and ensured the exported PDF output more closely reflects the on-screen report.

  • Baseline comparison now displayed directly inside reports
  • PDF exports now include delivery signal diagnostics
  • Improved narrative consistency between on-screen reports and exported PDFs
  • Refined PDF layout for clearer presentation of diagnostic findings
  • Improved alignment between dashboard reports and client-facing exports
v1.12 • APR 2026

Transparency and documentation improvements

Added public documentation pages to explain how iQWEB diagnostics and scoring work.

  • New FAQ explaining common questions about the platform
  • New Methodology page describing how signals and AI Visibility are evaluated
  • Improves transparency around how reports and scoring are generated
v1.13 • APR 2026

Simplified pricing and flexible plans

Updated the pricing model to make iQWEB easier to adopt with clearer plans and lower entry cost.

  • Introduced simplified Freelancer and Agency plans
  • Lowered entry pricing to make diagnostic reporting more accessible
  • Added Custom plan option for teams needing higher report volume
  • Improved plan structure to better support freelancers, agencies, and growing teams
v1.14 • APR 2026

AI Visibility report clarity improvements

Improved how AI Visibility results are presented in reports so category detection and recommendation test outcomes are visually clearer.

  • Category detection panel now highlights successful category identification
  • Recommendation test results now visually indicate when a brand is surfaced
  • Improved colour logic for AI Visibility panels to better reflect successful signals
  • Refined report layout to make AI visibility outcomes easier to interpret
