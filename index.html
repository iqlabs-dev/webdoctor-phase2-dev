What iQWEB checks before generating the report

Performance

How fast your pages load and respond for real users.

  • Core Web Vitals evaluation
    (LCP, INP, CLS)
  • Real-world load behaviour and responsiveness
  • Asset weight and request efficiency
  • Server response and TTFB patterns

SEO Foundations

How clearly search engines understand and rank your site.

  • Title, meta and canonical clarity
  • Indexability and crawl readiness
  • Robots directives and indexability signals

AI Visibility

Whether AI systems recognize and recommend the website as a trusted source for solutions in its category.

  • Category-level AI recommendations
  • Independent brand mentions and citations
  • Knowledge graph and authority signals

Structure & Semantics

Whether your HTML structure communicates meaning.

  • Heading hierarchy
  • Core landmarks
    (header, main, nav, footer)
  • Missing or broken structural elements

Mobile Experience

How your site behaves on the screens your customers actually use.

  • Responsive layout integrity
  • Tap target sizing, spacing and usability
  • Readability on small devices

Security & Trust

Security checks that protect users and reinforce site trust.

  • Protocol and redirect behaviour
  • Key security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options)
  • Mixed-content and downgrade risks

Accessibility

How usable your site is for people using assistive technologies.

  • Contrast, labels and ARIA usage
  • Alt text coverage for key imagery
  • Keyboard and screen reader traps

Content & Metadata

Whether pages present clear relevance and preview signals.

  • Thin or near-empty pages
  • Content completeness indicators
  • Social preview and Open Graph hygiene

Client-Ready Reports

What your client receives after the scan completes.

  • Clear executive summary
  • Prioritised “Top Issues” breakdown
  • Prioritized action plan ranked by impact & effort
  • Step-by-step recommended fixes
  • Baseline comparison across scans

Platform-Aware Scoring

iQWEB detects when a site is built on a managed platform and adjusts scoring to reflect what is actually within developer control. Infrastructure elements such as hosting, security headers, and server configuration may be controlled by the platform rather than the site owner. These signals are interpreted in context and are not treated as direct actionable issues.
